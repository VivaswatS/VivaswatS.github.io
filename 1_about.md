I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Computational Genomics at [AbbVie](https://www.abbvie.com/science/areas-of-innovation/genomics.html) in North Chicago. 

Briefly, I am a problem-solver at heart and enjoy answering biological questions through data-driven computational and statistical techniques. To this end, I have developed three software packages (in C++, python and R) for wider use in the community, with over 25,000 downloads in total. I thrive in collaborative environments and enjoy working across fields to decipher shared patterns. You can find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vivaswat-shastry/) too!   

Outside of work, I like meeting new people and making them laugh. On days when I'm blessed by the _Divine Providence_, I attempt writing these jokes down for wider dissemination.
